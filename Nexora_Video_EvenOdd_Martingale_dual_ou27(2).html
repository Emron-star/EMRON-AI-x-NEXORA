Questions

Is this an official Deriv product?

No. It is an independent tool that connects to your Deriv account through Deriv's public API.

Where does my token go?

You paste a scoped personal access token. It is used to ask Deriv for your Options accounts and a one-time WebSocket address, is kept in page memory only, and is cleared on disconnect or tab close.

Does any strategy guarantee profit?

No. Every Deriv contract is priced with a margin, so over many trades the expected result is a loss of roughly that margin on everything staked, whatever the staking plan. Recovery staking changes the shape of results, not the expectation.

Demo or real?

Start on demo. Real accounts need a stop loss above zero and a typed confirmation before trading starts.
